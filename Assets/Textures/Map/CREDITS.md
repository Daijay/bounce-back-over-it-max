# Map texture credits

No third-party pack was used. All textures in this folder were generated by us
with seeded scripts (first a Python one, now C# in the Editor), so they are our own work, released as CC0 / public domain.

Packs attempted: none. The time-boxed search for CC0 packs (kenney.nl, opengameart.org, itch.io)
was skipped, nothing was downloaded, and nothing was blocked.

Generated by us (CC0 / own work): rock, ice, sticky, pad, gold, crust, lava_0..3, plank, rope,
wood_post, sign_post, sign_board_plain, sign_board_arrow_left, flame_0..3, bubble_0..3, hanging_plank.

Notes:
- One texture pixel = one rat pixel in world size. Textures are drawn at the rat's pixel density.
- To swap a texture, replace the PNG with one of the same name and the same pixel size.

- grass_top, grass_fill and grass_tuft (the green start field and the tufts over the rock edge, 4 greens) are generated by `MapGrass` / `MapTextureGen.Grass*` (`BounceBack > Build Map` writes them), our own work, CC0.
  No external images. (Only grass_tuft is still used; a `grass.png` is no longer read.)
- ground_field (grass, dirt, stone layers on the curved mountain foot), pad_look and pad_arrow (bounce pad), snow, force_field (red boundary bars), trail_body and trail_head (dark blue snow trail), plus the ice and snow pieces and the one-way lips, are generated by MapArt / MapGrass / MapCaps (BounceBack > Build Map), our own work, CC0. No external images.
  (grass_top, grass_fill, tree_trunk, tree_pine_0..2 and tree_leaf from earlier versions are no longer written or used and can be deleted.)
- golden_chest and chest_sparkle (the treasure chest) are generated by MapTextureGen (BounceBack > Build Map), our own work, CC0. No external images.
- fx_disc, fx_ring, fx_smoke, fx_halo (the eruption) are generated by MapArt (BounceBack > Build Map), our own work, CC0. trail_body and trail_head are no longer written or used.
- asteroid_0..4_0..5 (the sky asteroids), cheese_moon and moon_glow, space_gradient, nebula_0..2, milky_way, star_glint, planet_0..4 (MapSpaceArt), space_star, ember, sign_bubble are generated by MapTextureGen / MapArt / MapGrass (BounceBack > Build Map), our own work, CC0. No external images.
- volcano_backdrop and volcano_glow (dark gray volcano interior with a warm banded gradient, faint glow on the pit walls) are generated by `MapVolcano` (`BounceBack > Build Map`), our own work, CC0. No external images.
- The Background sky and mountains are cut from background_main.png (the picture of this project, its moon removed); the moon layer picture 02_moon.png is no longer written.
- The background forest (four tree layers in `Assets/Sprites/Background/Layers`) is generated by `BackgroundTreeMaker`, our own work, CC0. No external images.
- rock and lava_0..3 were redrawn calmer by `BounceBack > Calm Map Textures` (`Assets/Editor/MapTextureGen.cs`, our own work, CC0):
  lava = fewer, larger blobs, bright shade rare. Both tile seamlessly.
- rock redrawn as a rock face (chunky stone blocks, dark crevices, lighter top edges, 5 close brown gray shades) and boulder_s0..2 / boulder_m0..2 / boulder_l0..2 (jagged convex stone chunks with facets,
  lit top left plane, dark bottom right plane, cracks; the collider uses the same outline) are generated by `MapTextureGen` (`BounceBack > Calm Map Textures`), our own work, CC0. No external images.
